Jungle Oats

A platform game made way back in 2005 with Freehand MX and Flash MX. Swing, jump and grab the oats.

About the game

Jungle Oats was drawn in Macromedia Freehand MX and brought to life in Flash MX. It is a small piece of web history, kept playable for anyone who remembers the Flash era.
